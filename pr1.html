Department Overview

K. K. Wagh Institute of Engineering Education and Research was established in 1984 in Nashik, Maharashtra. The institute provides quality technical education.

The Information Technology Department focuses on programming, software development, database management and modern technologies.

Course: B.Tech in Information Technology

Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters)

Vision

To be a valuable resource for industry and society through quality education and research in engineering and management.

Mission

  • Provide quality education to students.
  • Promote research and development.
  • Encourage innovation to meet society's needs.
  • Develop technical skills and professional qualities.
